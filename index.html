Featured Projects

VTB — Vote Through Blockchain

Backend & Web3 Engineer | Hackathon Award Winner

Designed and deployed a decentralized voting application that leverages blockchain technology to enhance transparency and prevent double-voting. The core challenge wasn't just building a smart contract—it was solving the voter privacy paradox: how do you prove someone voted *without* revealing *who* they voted for?

We implemented an HMAC-SHA256 nullifier scheme that allows voters to prove participation anonymously. The platform is currently under active development and approaching market release.

What I learned: how to design cryptographic systems for real-world constraints, the full cycle of blockchain development (Solidity, ethers.js, testnet deployment), and how to communicate complex technical ideas under pressure (20-minute Pecha Kucha presentation).

REDES — Intergenerational Mentorship Platform

Co-founder | Hackathon Participant (Iberdrola, La Nave)

Built a platform to connect young talent with senior professionals at Iberdrola, addressing youth unemployment through structured mentorship. The insight was simple: young people don't lack potential—they lack access to experienced guides and real opportunities.

What struck me most was how a small technical system could unlock human value. This project reinforced my belief that technology's real purpose is enabling people, not replacing them.

What I learned: the importance of understanding user needs deeply (we interviewed dozens of young people about their barriers to employment), and how to ship an MVP under time pressure while keeping the social mission intact.
